# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). The summary below is for convenience; the linked text is the standard that applies.

## Our standards

Everyone taking part in this project, in issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other project space, is expected to:

- be respectful and patient, including with people who are new to hardware or to Rust
- give and accept technical criticism about the work, not the person
- assume good faith, and say so plainly when something is wrong
- take responsibility for mistakes and fix them

The following are not acceptable:

- harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- discriminatory language or imagery of any kind
- publishing someone else's private information without permission
- sustained disruption of discussions

## Reporting

If you see or experience behavior that breaks these standards, contact the maintainer privately through the details on their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/jackulau). Reports are kept confidential.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues, and other contributions that break this code of conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban a contributor for behavior they judge to be harmful. Enforcement follows the [Contributor Covenant enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
